Calculate straight-pipe wall, pipe mass, bends, miter bends and pressure-vessel components. Organize projects, record site progress and generate PDF reports. PiCal brings calculation inputs, equations and results together in one place.
Calculation references
ASME B31.3-2018: process piping, straight pipe, bends and miters.
ASME B31.1-2022: power piping, straight pipe and miter pressure paths/segment wall.
ASME B36.10-2022: the available nominal pipe dimensions.
ASME BPVC VIII Division 1: the implemented vessel-component rules; stress input from Section II-D and joint efficiency from UW-12. The BPVC edition is not independently established by the supplied excerpts.
Using the results
Equations and code references appear in each calculator and its report. Use S for the actual material/product and design metal temperature. Automatic S is limited to verified B31.1 A106 seamless grades A/B/C; other cases require a manual table value. Results cover the implemented equations and their scope, and do not constitute complete code qualification.
