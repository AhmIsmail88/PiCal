Design equation and code reference
ASME B31.1-2022, 104.1.2(a), (7)
tm = P Do / [2 (S E W + P y)] + A
A = corrosion + erosion + mechanical
Pressure equations use MPa: input bar × 0.1 = MPa. D, R and wall dimensions are in mm; S is MPa. E/W/Y are unitless. Miter equations produce Pm in MPa; the screen converts it to bar.
B31.1-2022, 104.1.2(a), Eq. (7), verified against the supplied p.27: tm = P Do / [2(SEW + Py)] + A. Enter S before E/F; do not apply the quality factor twice to a combined SE/SF table value.
